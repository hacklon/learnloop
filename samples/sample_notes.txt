Class 8A - Biology - Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is how green plants make their own food. It happens mainly in the leaves.
Word equation: carbon dioxide + water --(sunlight, chlorophyll)--> glucose + oxygen.
Remember: plants take IN carbon dioxide and give OUT oxygen during the day.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment inside chloroplasts. It traps sunlight energy.
That is why leaves look green: chlorophyll reflects green light and absorbs red and blue light.

Stomata are tiny pores on the underside of the leaf. Carbon dioxide enters and oxygen leaves through them.
Guard cells open and close the stomata. They usually close at night and in very hot weather to save water.

Water reaches the leaf from the roots through the xylem.
Glucose made in the leaf is stored as starch. Iodine test: starch turns blue-black.
